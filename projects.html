Formulated a non-linear exponential model, y = 1966.957 x 1.015^x, with an R² value of 0.995 using 12 years of production data to forecast long-term warehouse storage requirements. Transformed mathematical models into computable polynomials and applied root-finding algorithms to predict when inventory would exceed the 25,000-unit capacity, including a 13-month construction buffer for facility expansion planning.

Developed a smart IoT cane to help people with visual impairments detect obstacles and move more safely through crowded environments and across roads. The ESP32-based system combines ultrasonic and infrared sensors with sensor fusion, median/sliding-window filtering, and adaptive thresholds to reduce noise and false alarms. It provides graded vibration feedback, optional buzzer alerts, and an IoT-ready foundation for Wi-Fi monitoring and telemetry.
